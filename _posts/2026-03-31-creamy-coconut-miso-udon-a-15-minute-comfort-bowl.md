# Creamy Coconut Miso Udon

When you need comfort in a bowl but only have fifteen minutes to spare, this **Creamy Coconut Miso Udon** is the ultimate answer. It is a fusion of deep, savory Japanese miso and the rich, velvety texture of coconut milk. The result is a soul-warming broth that clings perfectly to thick, chewy udon noodles.

### Why You’ll Love This Recipe
- **Pantry Friendly:** Most ingredients are likely already in your kitchen.
- **One Pot:** Minimal cleanup required.
- **Plant-Based:** Naturally vegan and incredibly satisfying.

### Ingredients
- 2 packs of frozen or fresh Udon noodles
- 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 2 tbsp white or yellow miso paste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 cup fresh spinach or bok choy
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (optional)
- **Toppings:** Scallions, chili oil, toasted sesame seeds

### Instructions
1. **Prepare the Noodles:** Cook the udon noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside. (If using frozen udon, a quick 1-minute blanch is usually all they need).
2. **Sauté Aromatics:** In a medium pot over medium heat, sauté the garlic and ginger with a splash of oil until fragrant (about 1 minute).
3. **Build the Broth:** Pour in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer. In a small bowl, whisk the miso paste with a few tablespoons of the warm coconut milk until smooth, then stir it back into the pot. This prevents miso clumps!
4. **Combine:** Add your greens (spinach or bok choy) to the broth until wilted. Toss in the cooked udon noodles and stir until the noodles are well-coated and heated through.
5. **Serve:** Divide into bowls and top generously with sliced scallions, a drizzle of chili oil for heat, and sesame seeds.

### Tips for Success
- **Don't Boil the Miso:** Avoid bringing the soup to a rolling boil once the miso is added, as high heat can destroy the delicate fermented flavor and nutritional benefits of the paste.
- **Add Protein:** This base works beautifully with pan-fried tofu, sautéed mushrooms, or even a soft-boiled jammy egg.

This recipe is the perfect solution for busy weeknights when you want something that tastes like it took hours to simmer, but actually came together in the time it took to boil water. Enjoy!
